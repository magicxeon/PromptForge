# Architectural Lean - Stage 1 Prompt

**Dataset:** 10 images  
**Recipe role:** Pose and environment composition  
**Status:** Pilot candidate; not production-qualified

```text
Create one photorealistic vertical on-location fashion photograph. Show one subject making clear, physically believable contact with a real architectural surface such as a concrete wall, plaster column, tiled wall, or structural support. Build the pose around asymmetric weight distribution: the supporting side remains stable while the free knee bends, crosses lightly, or rests against the surface. Keep the shoulders relaxed, the body line elongated, and the hands restrained through one pocket, a relaxed arm, or one deliberate point of contact. Use an eye-level or subtly low camera with a full-body or three-quarter composition and enough surrounding architecture to make the lean readable. Let wall planes, columns, seams, and directional daylight create clean geometry and depth. Preserve natural balance, visible hands when included, realistic fabric behavior, grounded feet, and a professional editorial fashion mood. Avoid floating contact, impossible joints, random props, busy signage, duplicate subjects, and an artificial studio void.
```

Controlled variations: full-body versus three-quarter crop; hard geometric sun
versus soft directional daylight; wall, column, or structural support.
