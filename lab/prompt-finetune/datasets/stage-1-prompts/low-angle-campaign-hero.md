# Low-angle Campaign Hero - Stage 1 Prompt

**Dataset:** 7 images  
**Recipe role:** Camera and campaign composition  
**Status:** Pilot candidate; not production-qualified

```text
Create one vertical high-fashion campaign photograph from a camera positioned clearly below the subject and tilted upward. Make the subject feel dominant without distorting anatomy: preserve a believable head-to-body relationship, natural hands, and stable legs while allowing mild foreground perspective. Place the body against clean sky, restrained architecture, or a simple studio field that leaves intentional negative space around the head and upper body. Use a confident upright stance or controlled editorial gesture, with the chin neutral to slightly lifted and the gaze directed away, toward the horizon, or calmly toward the camera. Keep the clothing silhouette readable and the frame uncluttered. Use crisp directional daylight or controlled studio light with deliberate edge separation. Avoid extreme fisheye distortion, cropped joints, accidental under-chin emphasis, unrelated props, duplicate subjects, and chaotic backgrounds.
```

Controlled variations must split into `full-body hero` and `medium portrait
hero`; the dataset does not support forcing one crop across both clusters.
