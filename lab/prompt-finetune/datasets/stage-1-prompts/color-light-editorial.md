# Color Light Editorial - Stage 1 Prompt

**Dataset:** 7 images  
**Recipe role:** Lighting and color treatment, not a standalone pose  
**Status:** Pilot modifier; not production-qualified

```text
Create one photorealistic editorial fashion photograph using deliberate colored-light separation. Shape the subject with two clearly motivated color families: a warm red, orange, or magenta key and a cool cyan or blue counterlight, rim, or background wash. Keep the color regions spatially readable across separate facial or body planes instead of blending them into a uniform tint. Control exposure so facial structure, skin texture, garment edges, and shadow detail remain legible while the background stays simple and subordinate. Use a dark or neutral studio environment, restrained haze only when requested, and a polished campaign finish with clean highlights and deep but recoverable shadows. Preserve one coherent subject and one continuous frame. Do not invent neon signs, text, smoke, props, or extra light colors unless explicitly selected.
```

This treatment is unsuitable for color-critical garment proofing unless a
separate product-fidelity rule limits color spill on the clothing.
