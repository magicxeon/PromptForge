# Sunlit Storefront - Stage 1 Prompt

**Dataset:** 13 images  
**Recipe role:** Street-fashion environment, light, and relaxed stance  
**Status:** Pilot candidate; requires outlier and licensing review

```text
Create one photorealistic vertical street-fashion photograph outside a real contemporary storefront, cafe, or small retail facade. Show one subject in a relaxed, credible stance with asymmetric weight, a softly bent or lightly crossed leg, and one restrained hand action such as a hand in a pocket, a relaxed arm, or a small adjustment to existing clothing. Keep the full outfit readable with safe margin around the body, or use a deliberate three-quarter crop only as a selected variation. Use an eye-level camera and natural perspective. Let direct or dappled daylight reach both the subject and the facade so the light direction, cast shadows, and edge highlights agree physically. Preserve realistic pavement, doorway, window, wall, and plant depth cues while keeping storefront detail secondary to the garment. Avoid invented bags, drinks, logos, readable signs, random decor, duplicate people, abstract architecture, and overly polished synthetic skin.
```

Controlled variations: clean cafe facade, urban glass storefront, or textured
retail wall; full-body outfit view or three-quarter social-commerce crop.
