```text
Create one photorealistic identity-first beauty portrait framed from the shoulders or upper torso upward. Turn the shoulders slightly away from the camera and let the head return gently toward the lens, or use a calm near-frontal pose when identity clarity is the priority. Keep the expression quiet and natural, with direct or near-direct eye connection. Use one broad soft directional source from a window or large diffused light, a clear but gentle eye catchlight, restrained contrast, and smooth highlight roll-off. Preserve visible natural skin texture, fine hair detail, believable facial asymmetry, and a softly separated neutral or real interior background. Keep the hands outside the crop unless one restrained gesture is intentionally selected. Avoid passport stiffness, plastic skin, excessive beauty retouching, floating hair, text, duplicate faces, and decorative props that compete with identity.
```
